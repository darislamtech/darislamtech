# Project rules

## Build for growth — the owner's first rule (owner's rule, added by claude)
The project will grow very large: many pages and features, a backend and accounts later, and many developers and agents working on it. We build it organized from the start, so that whoever opens the code in a year finds where everything is and why, without guessing or untangling a mess. Every agent follows this on every change. It comes first among the working rules and conventions below; the priority order in `INDEX.md` still applies (correctness, security and the business rules come before structure). It applies to the code a task touches: restructuring outside the task is proposed to the owner, never done on the side.
- **One clear home for each kind of thing.** Pages in `src/app/[lang]/<page>/`; site content as typed data in `src/content/`; every visible text in the two dictionaries; components grouped by feature (`src/components/<feature>/`), with only shared building blocks at the root of `components/` (listed in `ARCHITECTURE.md`); helpers in `src/lib/`; site settings in `src/config/`. A new kind of thing gets its home decided once (a new top-level folder or pattern is proposed to the owner first) and written in `ARCHITECTURE.md` in the same change.
- **Small, focused, well-named.** One job per file, component and function; split a file once it does two jobs. Names say what a thing is or does. Named constants and design tokens, never magic values.
- **Build once, use everywhere — when it is truly the same thing.** Build a thing once as a shared piece and use it in every place that needs it (like the one YouTube player used by every video on the site), rather than a separate version for each place. Before adding, look for an existing piece and extend it. Share what has the same meaning and changes for the same reasons; things that only look alike but serve different purposes stay separate, because a wrong shared abstraction is worse than a little duplication.
- **Typed and explicit.** Every piece of data has a type; no `any`; no hidden coupling between files (such as reading another component's DOM or relying on another file's internals) without a comment saying why.
- **Nothing left behind.** No dead code, commented-out code, unused exports, dictionary keys or files, and no temporary hacks. If a shortcut is unavoidable, say so in the report and record the follow-up on the task.
- **The site stays current.** Nothing on the site may stay outdated: the current phase, counts, "coming soon" labels, descriptions and plans. On every change, check the pages it touches and the pages that summarize them (the home page cards and sections, the footer, meta descriptions); fix what the change made outdated, and report anything else found out of date to the owner without waiting to be asked (owner's rule).
- **The map matches the code.** `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/` and these rules are updated in the same change as the code they describe; a change is not done until they match.
- **Judgment first: these points are guides, not ends in themselves.** The base is senior software engineering: clean code and the design patterns that fit the problem. When applying a point literally would make the code worse (a forced abstraction, a needless split, an over-general component), do what a senior engineer would do and say why in the report.
- **Reorganize before it gets messy.** When a folder or file grows too large, or a structure no longer fits, propose a reorganization to the owner instead of piling more on top.

## Business rules (from the owner's decisions in Notion)
- Dar Islam & Technology is free: no ads, and no paid products, courses or subscriptions. It is not described as "non-profit" / «غير ربحية»: support pays the costs and a fair margin for the founder, so he can work on it full time (owner's decision, added by claude). Financial support is optional and lives only on the support page (`/support`), through the founder's PayPal.Me, Buy Me a Coffee and Patreon links (never a PayPal "Donate" button). Its wording is "support the project" / "support the content": never "donation" (تبرع) or "charity" (صدقة), and no WhatsApp contact for payments.
- Never add explanations of the initiative's legal status to the site (charity, official registration, business licences); the code licence (MIT) is fine.
- All products are free and open source. Never mark a product `available` before it actually launches; product statuses are `available`, `inDevelopment`, `comingSoon`, `idea`.
- "Learn" is under construction: it shows the program plan (an Islamic knowledge and da'wah track: what no Muslim may be ignorant of, then a da'wah project defending Islam, studied in depth with a content project; a technology track: foundation then advanced; no timing, each learner goes at their own pace, except the reading time of a book series; «ما لا يسع المسلمَ جهلُه» is studied from Zad Academy's book series level by level (each level's seven books in order, with what each covers; reading time in hours and weeks, no page or word counts), with a link to enroll at Zad, and each book with «قراءة» and «تحميل» links to its PDF on the darislamtech Google Drive under Zad Academy's written permission of 2026-11-12, whose terms are shown above the levels: free, for learning only, never sold, translated or printed (the Arabic page links the Arabic first edition, the English page Zad Group's English edition); each da'wah field may list YouTube videos to start with under «ابدأ من هنا», playing in place), in two tabs, «الخطة التعليمية بمصادر خارجية» (recommended outside sources; the one being worked on) and «الخطة التعليمية من المنصة» (Dar's own lessons; frozen on the earlier plan for now, changed only when the owner asks, and shown blurred and inert under a «جارٍ تخطيطها وإنتاجها» card), and no lessons until real material exists (plan updated at the owner's request, claude). This plan is the owner's initial idea: never describe it on the site as an "initial idea".
- Videos stay on YouTube and are embedded only, added by hand (no automatic import).
- Entertainment videos show only the YouTube title and thumbnail (no description; DarTube adds a line with the channel and views, like YouTube's home page); the player loads in the page on click (`components/video/video-card.tsx`, youtube-nocookie).
- Entertainment videos: each entry carries `added` (when we added it). The category with the newest addition comes first; "newest/oldest" sort by `added`, "most viewed" by YouTube views (`getYouTubeDetails`). Each category is one row (`components/entertainment/video-rows.tsx`): 3 per view with arrows on large screens, swipe on phones (owner's decision, added by claude). The "most viewed" button is hidden when no video has a view count (owner's choice, added by claude).
- Entertainment has three pages with the same two groups (tabs), each type with its own categories: videos (`/entertainment`), channels (`/entertainment/channels`, YouTube only; the card is like YouTube's channel lists: round picture and the name as written on the channel, no description or subscriber count; it opens the channel) and books (`/entertainment/books`; cover and title, plus the author for Islamic books only; no link, since many books circulate without their authors' permission) (owner's decision, added by claude). A fourth page, DarTube «دار تيوب» (`/entertainment/dartube`), shows every recommended video once, both groups together and with no tabs or categories, in a grid like YouTube's home page, in a new random order on every visit (shuffled in the browser; a video still playing keeps its place) (owner's decision, added by claude).
- The owner sends a channel, book or video; the agent picks its category from the item's actual content (the channel's description and latest videos, the book's subject) and creates a new category when none fits. A video whose category is unclear goes to «منوعات» (`misc`). Keep the owner's order inside a category (owner's rule, added by claude).
- The founder («مروان حجازي — المؤسس والمشرف») and his personal accounts (`marwan7egazy`) appear only where he asked: the «المؤسس» section of the About page (with the bio he gave), the contact card of the support page, his PayPal.Me, Buy Me a Coffee and Patreon links in the support page's money section, and his YouTube channel among the recommended channels (and his videos when he sends them) (owner's request, added by claude). No photo unless he sends one.
- Only link accounts and channels that exist (`src/config/site.ts`, listed in `docs/accounts.md`). No Snapchat.
- Islamic content needs trusted sources and qualified review; never add verses, adhkar or rulings from unverified sources.
- Design decisions (logo, colors, fonts, page layouts) are discussed with the owner before they are treated as approved.

## Working rules — lessons from past mistakes (owner's rule, added by claude)
Each rule below exists because the mistake already happened once. Add a new one whenever a mistake is found.
- If any part of a request is unclear (what to change, where, or how much), ask the owner to clarify before doing anything; never act on a guess.
- Change only what was asked. Edit text by its exact key or line; never run a broad pattern (regex/sed) across a dictionary, because the same words appear on pages that must not change (a broad replace once renamed a home-page card).
- Before every commit, read the full `git diff` and confirm each changed line was intended (a stale edit once brought back a phrase that had just been removed).
- When the owner points at a list or table and says «احذفهم» / "delete them", it applies to every item shown. If it is unclear which items or which element (title or body) the owner means, ask before acting.
- Use the owner's wording verbatim, in the exact element they point to.
- Inline Arabic text in separate elements next to each other must be separated by a space or a flex gap; otherwise letters join across elements (a final «ي» once rendered in its medial form).
- Header and nav: check English at 1024px and 1280px, not only Arabic; English labels are longer and once pushed a button off screen.
- After a change, remove any text the change made untrue (for example "coming soon" for something that is now live).
- Check the changed parts in the built site (screenshot or HTML) before reporting them as done.
- Take layout screenshots at a normal window height (at least 600px). Headless Edge lays out very short windows (~90px) wrongly, which once looked like a header overflow and led to reverting a correct fix. Compare with another page or the live site before concluding something is broken.
- Label every open PR you mention: «لا تدمجه بعد» while its review runs, «جاهز للدمج» only after approval; for stacked PRs say which one merges first. A stacked PR once got merged and deployed before its review finished.
- Write commit messages and PR bodies with the file-writing tool, not PowerShell `Set-Content -Encoding utf8` (Windows PowerShell 5.1 adds a BOM that ended up in a commit subject).
- Before relying on a browser event or API, read its documented lifecycle and test repeats, not only the first event (Chrome fires `beforeinstallprompt` again after its install dialog is closed; the install card once came back 3 s after the visitor dismissed it).
- After browser or server tests, stop every process the test started (headless Edge, `next start`) and check the process list; 68 headless Edge processes and four servers were once left running.

## Mobile — check on every change (owner's rule, added by claude)
- Mobile first: check every changed page at phone width (≈375px) in Arabic and English and in both themes. No horizontal page scroll, touch targets at least 44px, and grids that collapse to one column. Give `next/image` a real `sizes`.
- Report in the final summary which mobile widths were checked.
- Header icon buttons (theme, language, menu) look 36px on phones, and an invisible `before:` layer gives each a 44px touch area; keep 8px between them so the areas don't overlap (chosen by claude under the owner's delegation). The English logo and the three buttons must still fit at 320px.
- Measure horizontal scroll by comparing `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` with the emulated device width. With mobile emulation the layout viewport grows to fit wide content, so `scrollWidth > innerWidth` never fails; that check once missed a 4px overflow on every English page at 320px and a links bar wider than the phone.

## SEO — build every page on these basics (owner's rule, added by claude)
SEO matters to the owner. Apply these on every new or changed page:
- Every page exports `generateMetadata` with its own title and description from the dictionaries (both locales). Write them for people, with the words visitors actually search for and no keyword stuffing. Titles follow the layout template `Page | دار الإسلام والتقنية`.
- One `h1` per page; headings follow order (`h2` under it, `h3` under that), never skipped for styling.
- The brand name «دار الإسلام والتقنية» stays readable as text. The logo SVG is decorative (`aria-hidden`); the logo link's accessible name contains the brand name.
- Content that should be indexed is rendered on the server. Interactive parts (tabs, players) receive server-rendered content and only hide or show it, never fetch it on the client.
- Meaningful images get descriptive `alt` text; decorative ones get `alt=""`.
- Internal links use `next/link` with descriptive link text; URLs are short readable English slugs (`/entertainment`, `/products/<slug>`).
- Use semantic elements (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `section`, `article`, `footer`) and keep `<html lang dir>` correct per locale.
- Every page's `generateMetadata` returns `pageMetadata()` from `src/lib/seo.ts` (canonical, ar/en/x-default alternates, Open Graph, X card); `metadataBase` is `siteConfig.url`. A new page is also added to the `paths` list in `src/app/sitemap.ts`.
- The share image is `public/og-image.png` (1200×630), set by `pageMetadata` on every page (not the opengraph-image file convention: a page's own openGraph object drops inherited file images). The home page carries the Organization and WebSite JSON-LD.
- Entertainment pages (DarTube included) name their contents in the meta description (video topics, first 6 channels, first 6 books) and carry CollectionPage/ItemList JSON-LD: VideoObject (needs a title and uploadDate, else the video is left out; its description is the title, never the YouTube description, which the page does not show), channels by name and URL, Book with author and cover when present (owner approved, added by claude).

## Security headers (added by claude)
- Security headers and the Content-Security-Policy live in `next.config.ts`. Anything that loads from another origin (a script, iframe, font, analytics or API) must be added to the CSP in the same change, then checked on the built site.
- The CSP is sent on `darislamtech.com` only, so Vercel preview deployments keep their toolbar.
- The repository is public. GitHub secret scanning, push protection, Dependabot alerts and Dependabot security updates are on (owner approved, 2026-11-06); never turn them off. Secrets live only in Vercel environment variables (`.env*` is git-ignored; a future `.env.example` needs a `!.env.example` exception).
- Server secrets: `YOUTUBE_API_KEY` (Vercel, production + preview, sensitive; restricted to the YouTube Data API v3). Never put a secret in a fetch URL: Next stores fetch URLs in its Data Cache, so send keys in a header (a key in the URL was once written to `.next/cache/fetch-cache` in plain text, caught in review before push).

## Documentation — `docs/` (owner's rule, added by claude)
- `docs/` is the organized explanation of the site, written in Arabic: `README.md` (index), `pages.md`, `content.md`, `design.md`, `languages-seo-pwa.md`, `accounts.md`.
- Every change to the site updates the affected doc in the same change, following `core/reporting.md`: docs describe the current state only, so outdated text is rewritten or deleted, never kept as history.
- A new page, content type or feature gets its section in the matching doc. A new doc is listed in `docs/README.md`. A doc whose subject no longer exists is deleted and removed from the index.
- `docs/` describes what the site is and how to use it; binding rules (must / must not) live in `RULES.md` and file locations in `ARCHITECTURE.md`. When a value appears in both (for example a color), change both in the same edit.

## Conventions
- Site copy: Islamic knowledge and da'wah always come before technology in every title, list and ordering. The Islamic side addresses the Muslim; the tech side addresses every person (e.g. «ما لا يسع المسلمَ جهلُه في دينه، وما لا يسع الإنسانَ جهلُه في التقنية»). Keep the current style: simple, warm Modern Standard Arabic. The approved definition, mission, vision and ambition are in `docs/README.md`.
- Git history (owner's rule): when the owner asks for a change and later reverts or reworks it, the history must look as if only the final version was ever written. Rework unpushed commits (amend or fixup) instead of adding "undo" commits, keep commit messages describing only what the commit contains, and recommend **Squash and merge** for PRs whose branch went back and forth. Force-pushing a pushed branch still needs the owner's approval.
- Every user-facing string goes in both `ar.json` and `en.json`; content entries carry `{ ar, en }`.
- Arabic is the default locale and must read correctly in RTL: use logical utilities (`ms-`, `pe-`, `start-`, `end-`), flip directional icons with `rtl:`, and wrap mixed-direction snippets with `<bdi>` or `dir`.
- No letter-spacing on Arabic text (it breaks letter joining); apply tracking with `ltr:` only.
- Colors only through the tokens in `globals.css`. No raw hex/rgb in components. Two themes, set by `data-theme` on `<html>`:
  - Light (default): white leaning very slightly grey #F7F7F8 (neutral, not warm or cream), cards #FDFDFD, charcoal text #29292B, calm green accent #3E6B53, gold buttons #DDA853 with charcoal text (same "gold = action" as the dark theme).
  - Dark: deep green page #17382F (glow #24483D), charcoal panels #29292B, cream text #F3F0E4. Gold #F0C982 only on buttons, the logo's door and ornaments; links and generic accents are blue #9EC0EE.
  - Four cheerful tones (owner: gold #F0C982, rose #FFBDB3, mint #A0E0C3, blue #9EC0EE) as `tone-gold|rose|mint|blue` (+ `-soft`). Every element takes its own tone so each page uses all four — not one tone per section. In the light theme the text uses a deep shade of the hue on a pastel fill. Use `Tone` / `toneText` / `toneDot` from `components/ui.tsx` and the `tone` props of `Badge`, `IconTile`, `Eyebrow`, `SectionHeading` (`eyebrowTone`) and `PageHero` (`eyebrowTone`).
  - The visitor's choice is stored in `localStorage` (`theme`) and applied before paint by `components/theme/theme-script.tsx`; the toggle is `components/theme/theme-toggle.tsx`. Use the `dark:` variant only for things tokens cannot express.
  - Page = `paper`; full-width alternate sections = `band`; cards = `surface` with `shadow-panel`; elements inside cards = `surface-2`.
  - Text = `ink` / `ink-2` / `muted`. Accent text, links and icons = `primary`; button fill = `primary-fill` with `on-primary` text (they differ in the light theme).
  - Primitive scales (`sand-*`, `emerald-*`, `gold-*`, `lapis-*`, `madder-*`) only for small decorative details.
  - Every new text/background pair must meet WCAG AA (4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text and UI marks) on every surface of both themes.
  - Product status colors come from `components/products/product-status.ts`; a product's icon takes its status color (owner's decision, added by claude).
- Logo: `components/brand.tsx`, traced from the owner's logo image (a house-shaped arch spelling «دار» with a gold door). Tokens `logo` (left half), `logo-deep` (right half) and `logo-accent` (door): greens #2A513F / #1C3A2F and gold #D0A368 in light, cream #F3F0E4 / #DDD8C6 and gold #F0C982 in dark. The favicon and app icons show the image's own colors on its cream #F8F5EB. `app/icon.svg`, `app/favicon.ico`, `app/apple-icon.png` and `public/icons/*` are generated from the same paths.
- PWA: `app/manifest.ts` (installable, opens on `/` so the proxy picks the visitor's language, standalone, Arabic name and RTL) with the icons in `public/icons/` (192, 512 and a maskable 512). The mobile install card is `components/layout/install-prompt.tsx` (Install button on Chromium once it fires `beforeinstallprompt`, Share → Add to Home Screen steps on iOS Safari but not in social in-app browsers, "remind me in a week" or closing the install dialog snoozes it 7 days in localStorage; the browser's own install banner stays held back on phones). No service worker yet: no offline mode and no notifications.
- Reuse primitives in `components/ui.tsx` and `components/page-blocks.tsx` before adding new ones.
- Animations must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Read the relevant guide in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before using a Next.js API.
